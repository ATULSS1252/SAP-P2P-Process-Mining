## Key Findings Preview
* **Overall Compliance Deviation:** Quantified deviation rate against the standard normative 3-way match path.
* **Rework Loops:** Identified high-frequency iteration loops in purchase order item modifications and invoice verifications.
* **Post-GR Modifications:** Tracked price and quantity changes occurring strictly after goods receipt.
* **Invoice Exceptions:** Highlighted clusters of duplicate and blocked invoices.
* **Subsidiary Spread:** Mapped the days-to-clear distribution variance across 60 distinct corporate subsidiaries.
